Privacy Policy for Shoppy

This Privacy Policy explains how Shoppy ("we", "us", or "our"), developed and operated by Santhosh Kannan, collects, stores, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

The Application collects and processes only the information necessary to provide e-commerce features:

  • Account & Profile Information: Username, password, and gender selection provided during signup and authentication.
  • Checkout & Delivery Information: Full name, contact phone number, shipping address, postal code, and payment method preferences entered during order checkout. (Note: We do not process or store actual payment card numbers or banking passwords).
  • Shopping & Activity Data: Search query strings, cart items, wishlist selections, and past order history.
  • Technical Diagnostics: Device IP address, operating system details, and network state headers transmitted via standard HTTP connections.

2. Data Not Collected

The Application does NOT access or collect:

  • Precise GPS location data
  • Device camera, contacts, or media storage
  • Advertising IDs (GAID/IDFA) or biometric records

3. Third-Party Services & APIs

We interact with trusted third-party providers strictly to facilitate app functionality:

  • E-Commerce Backend APIs: Standard REST APIs (such as DummyJSON) for product catalogs, search indexing, and sandbox authentication workflows.
  • Google Fonts: Typography rendering assets fetched via secure network calls.
  • Zero Ad/Analytics Trackers: We do not integrate third-party advertising SDKs or behavioural trackers.

4. On-Device Local Storage

To provide offline persistence, theme switching, and persistent login, data is saved locally on your device via SharedPreferences (e.g., authentication tokens, UI mode, cart cache, and order history). This data remains on your device until explicitly wiped.

5. Data Erasure & Account Deletion

You have full authority over your data:

  • Logout Action: Tapping "Logout" inside the app immediately purges local session tokens, user profile metadata, cart items, and stored orders.
  • Complete Data Erasure: Clearing app storage via Android Device Settings or uninstalling the app permanently deletes all stored preferences and cache.

6. Security

All network communications use secure TLS/HTTPS protocols. We enforce reasonable security practices to protect your data from unauthorized access or disclosure.

7. Children's Privacy

The Application is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

8. Contact Us

For questions or requests regarding your personal data and privacy, please contact the developer at: santhoshhcodes@gmail.com
